# FrameForge identity

Modern geometric video-frame and play-symbol mark. Existing orange (#ff841c), amber (#ffb52e), and charcoal (#25282c) palette. Transparent orange wordmark shared by light and dark themes. Production asset: `static/frameforge-logo.png`.

Created with the built-in image-generation tool. Final prompt:

> Create a fresh premium modern software logo for a local professional video converter named exactly "FrameForge". Transparent background. Horizontal compact wordmark with a geometric icon to the left. Icon combines a crisp angular video frame and play triangle, with subtle forge energy through an orange/amber geometric fold; no literal cartoon fire, no mascots, no tools. Restrained flat vector-like design, extremely clean edges, generous clearspace, highly legible precise sans-serif typography. Colors: orange #ff841c and warm amber #ffb52e with charcoal #25282c. Wordmark entirely orange #ff841c so it is readable on both light cream and dark charcoal interfaces. Exact text "FrameForge", capital F twice, no Andy, no subtitle, no slogan. Single logo only, not a mockup or branding sheet. Wide composition tightly centered, simple scalable shapes, genuine transparent alpha.
